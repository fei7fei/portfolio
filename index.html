03. Research

Submitted · SPIE Medical Imaging 2027 · Decision expected January 2027

Does Medical Pretraining Change What a Chest-Radiograph Classifier Needs? A Frozen-Protocol Comparison on CheXpert

Pei Lin (Michael) Xie and Yi-An Su · University of British Columbia Okanagan

Chest X-ray AI models are usually judged by accuracy, a number that can hide dangerously low disease detection. Evaluating the medical foundation model Ark+ on CheXpert, we found a model reaching 86% accuracy caught only about 3 in 10 true disease cases at the standard threshold. Validation-tuned threshold selection raised sensitivity to 0.73–0.84 with no retraining, and a targeted class-weighted loss improved detection of Atelectasis and Consolidation. Five more architectures are being compared under the same frozen protocol.

  • Swin Transformer
